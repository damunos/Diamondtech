## Moving business email to Microsoft 365

Start with an inventory of user mailboxes, shared addresses, aliases, and devices. Identify who manages the domain and where the current mail is hosted. Peter can help plan what should move, when to switch mail delivery, and how to check that staff can receive mail and see the folders they need afterward.

## Office Wi-Fi and network troubleshooting

If wireless service works at the front desk but drops in the warehouse, note the affected areas, devices, and times of day. If wired computers lose connectivity at the same time, that points the investigation in a different direction. For a planned move, a floor plan and a list of wired equipment make a useful starting point.

## Account access and new staff

When someone joins, changes roles, or leaves, review their licenses, shared mailboxes, and access to business files. Peter holds Microsoft Applied Skills credentials in Entra identity and access and Azure management. [See Peter’s credentials](/#credentials).

- The number of users or locations involved
- The system or account that needs attention
