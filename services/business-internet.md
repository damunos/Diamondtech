## Compare the connections available at your building

Two businesses on the same street can have different connection options. Start with the full service address, including the suite or building number. Peter can help compare available providers on upload and download speeds, installation costs, equipment fees, contract length, and the support offered during an outage.

## Decide what needs a backup

List the systems that must stay online when the main circuit goes down. A restaurant might prioritize its payment terminals and phones. An office might need access to cloud files and remote meetings. That list helps determine whether a backup line needs to carry the whole office or just a few devices.

A second connection is most useful when it can survive the failure that took down the first. Ask about the carrier path, cellular signal at the router, backup power, and how to test that traffic switches over and back.

## What to send for an availability check

- The service address and suite number
- Your current provider and the problem you want to solve
- Any opening date or contract renewal deadline
